Education for All

Every child in school. Every learner a future.

The Challenge

Education is a fundamental right and a cornerstone of social and economic development. Yet over 25 million children aged 5 to 16, about 35% of that age group, are out of school in Pakistan. National literacy stands at 60.7%, and only 52.8% for women, while 77% of ten-year-olds cannot read and understand a simple text. SOS Foundation works to close these gaps through inclusive, equitable and quality education and lifelong learning for children, youth and adults, upholding every child's constitutional right to education under Article 25-A.

Source: Pakistan Institute of Education, Pakistan Education Statistics 2023-24 and Public Financing in Education 2025-26; Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Census 2023.

SDG alignment: primarily SDG 4 (Quality Education); also SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 5 (Gender Equality) and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).

What We Do

Flagship Initiative: National University for Security Sciences (NUSS)

Our flagship higher education project is the National University for Security Sciences, Islamabad, whose charter has been approved by both the National Assembly and the Senate of Pakistan. NUSS is envisioned as a centre of excellence for higher education, research and professional development in security sciences and allied disciplines. It will soon begin operations and will develop skilled professionals and future leaders for Pakistan.

Scholarships and Higher Education Support

We provide scholarships and financial assistance to talented students facing financial hardship, helping them pursue education at school, college and university level.

Teacher Development

We build the capacity of educators through training workshops on modern pedagogy, 21st-century teaching skills and classroom management.

Out-of-School Children and Basic Education

We help out-of-school children return to learning through accelerated learning programmes, learning materials and partnerships with community-based schools.

Adult Literacy and Advocacy

Our adult literacy programme offers second-chance learning to adults with little or no formal education. Through public awareness walks, policy dialogues and national literacy campaigns, we also advocate for action on Pakistan's literacy and out-of-school challenges.

Girls' Education

We advocate for girls' right to education and work to remove the barriers that keep them out of school, including poverty, safety concerns, social norms and early dropout. Through campaigns, seminars and partnerships with academic institutions, we promote girls' education, leadership and careers in science and technology.

Educational Infrastructure

We support the establishment and renovation of schools and learning spaces in underserved areas, so that students can learn in safe and conducive environments.
