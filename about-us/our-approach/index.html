Our Approach

Our Strategic Goals

  1. 1Expand access to quality education, healthcare and safe drinking water for underserved communities, and reach more individuals and families directly across all programmes.
  2. 2Build self-reliance through skills development, livelihoods and economic empowerment, with a special focus on women and youth.
  3. 3Strengthen resilience of vulnerable communities against disasters, climate change and economic shocks.
  4. 4Deliver measurable impact through evidence-based programming, monitoring and transparent reporting.
  5. 5Grow partnerships with government, corporate, academic and international partners to diversify our funding base and scale proven solutions.
  6. 6Pilot innovation by testing new interventions that respond to emerging community needs.

How We Work

Community-led

We design interventions with communities, not just for them, to ensure local ownership and lasting impact.

Partnership-driven

We collaborate with federal and provincial government bodies, academia, the private sector and development agencies to scale what works.

Results-focused

We track outcomes, not just activities, and report transparently to our stakeholders.

Accountable

Our governance, financial management and reporting reflect the standards of a responsible corporate group.

Our Guiding Principles

Dignity first

Every individual we serve is treated as a partner, not a recipient of charity.

Do no harm

Our interventions are designed to protect communities and never create new risks.

Neutrality

We serve people solely on the basis of need, regardless of religion, ethnicity, gender or political affiliation.

Safeguarding

We are committed to protecting children, women and vulnerable adults in all our programmes.

Transparency

We use every rupee responsibly and are open about how resources are spent.

Our Commitment

We stand with communities in their journey from hardship to hope, working with compassion, acting with integrity, and building a more equitable, resilient and prosperous Pakistan, one community at a time.

SOS Foundation is the CSR arm of SOS Group of Companies, empowering underserved communities across Pakistan since 2017.
