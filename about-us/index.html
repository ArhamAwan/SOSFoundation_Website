About Us

Building a Better Tomorrow, Together

Who We Are

SOS Foundation is the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) arm of SOS Group of Companies. It was established in 2017 under SOS Pakistan (Pvt.) Ltd. as a not-for-profit organisation. We work to improve the lives of underserved and marginalised communities across Pakistan through sustainable, community-driven development.

We are backed by the long-term commitment of SOS Group and our Board of Trustees. This brings corporate discipline, accountability and continuity to our social development work. We work alongside communities, government departments, civil society organisations and international development partners, so that our efforts complement national and provincial priorities.

Our Journey

Education and workforce development have been at the heart of our work since 2017. Through scholarships, teacher training and support for higher education, we have helped young people from low-income households continue their studies and build pathways to dignified employment. Our flagship initiative, the National University for Security Sciences (NUSS), Islamabad, has had its charter approved by both the National Assembly and the Senate of Pakistan.

Alongside education, we have responded to urgent basic needs. We have installed reverse osmosis (RO) water filtration plants and hand pumps that bring safe drinking water to underserved communities, and we run hunger-relief initiatives that provide meals and rations to vulnerable families. Today our work spans nine thematic programmes, from health and climate action to women's empowerment and human rights.

Our Vision

A world where every person can live with dignity, opportunity and hope, free from poverty and hardship.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through sustainable, partnership-driven initiatives in education, healthcare, clean water and livelihoods, so that they can move from hardship to self-reliance.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We put people first, serving every community with empathy, respect and care for human dignity.

Integrity

We act with honesty, transparency and fairness, and we honour the trust our partners and communities place in us.

Accountability

We take responsibility for our decisions, our use of resources and our results, and we report openly to communities, donors and partners.

Inclusion

We reach those most often left behind, including women, children, persons with disabilities and remote communities, without discrimination of any kind.

Collaboration

We believe lasting change comes from working together with communities, government, the private sector and development partners.

Innovation

We seek creative, evidence-based solutions that deliver lasting impact and can grow to reach more people.

Sustainability

We design programmes that communities can own and carry forward, so that impact lasts beyond our direct involvement.
