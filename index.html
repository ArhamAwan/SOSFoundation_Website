Who We Are

SOS Foundation is the Corporate Social Responsibility arm of SOS Group of Companies. Since 2017, we have worked with communities, government, academia and development partners to improve the lives of underserved and marginalised people across Pakistan through sustainable, community-driven development.

Learn more about us →

Flagship Initiative

National University for Security Sciences (NUSS), Islamabad. Our flagship higher education project, NUSS has had its charter approved by both the National Assembly and the Senate of Pakistan. It is envisioned as a centre of excellence for higher education, research and professional development in security sciences and allied disciplines, and will soon begin operations.
